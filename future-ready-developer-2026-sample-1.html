AI Reality

AI changes the developer role, not the need for developers.

OLD

Old Way

Developer writes 100% code, manually creates boilerplate, copies errors, searches for fixes and repeats.

NEW

New Way

Developer designs, prompts, reviews, validates and integrates. AI generates boilerplate and accelerates repetitive work.

NEXT

Agent Era

AI agents can inspect codebases, read logs, open browsers, test flows and suggest fixes. Humans still own business context.
