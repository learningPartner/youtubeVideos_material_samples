The Real Problem

Why 90 Days Notice Period Becomes a Problem?

Recruiters often need fast closures. A long notice period can filter you before your technical skills are even checked.

⚡

Urgent joiners win calls

Many teams are hiring for immediate project needs, so 0 to 30 days candidates get faster recruiter attention.

🔎

Profiles get filtered

Naukri and LinkedIn searches often prioritize shorter notice periods, reducing visibility for 90-day candidates.

🚪

HR may reject early

The conversation can close before resume quality, project depth, or coding ability gets evaluated.

🏢

Only some companies wait

Planned hiring teams, large firms, and referral openings are more likely to accept longer timelines.

💎

Strong profiles still matter

Rare skills, senior experience, and strong interview performance can make companies wait.

Smart Framing
01

Search While Employed, But Be Smart

Do not casually fake 30 days if your official policy says 90 days. Use a realistic, negotiable frame that keeps the discussion open.

"My official notice period is 90 days, but I am discussing early release or buyout options. Realistically, I can try for 45 to 60 days."
1

Profile wording

Use "Notice Period: Negotiable" or "90 days, early release can be discussed."

2

Recruiter call

Lead with flexibility, buyout possibility, and realistic 45 to 60 day target.

3

Policy check

Before resigning, verify buyout, leave adjustment, and early release rules.

4

Offer strategy

If you have EMI, family responsibilities, or no backup money, crack one offer before resigning.

Practical Options

Six Ways To Manage The 3 Month Timeline

The right path depends on money pressure, preparation level, market demand, and your current company policy.

Safest

Get One Offer, Then Resign

Prepare strongly, attend interviews, crack one offer, then start the official notice period. Best for developers with financial responsibilities. if Interview Feedback is strong Company wait for 3 Months also

Policy Lever

Early Release Or Buyout

Many companies allow salary buyout, leave adjustment, or manager-approved release. Ask properly before assuming full 90 days is compulsory.

Risky

Resign First, Search Aggressively

Powerful only when preparation is strong, savings are ready, and pressure is low. As days reduce, recruiter interest improves. Most are Choose this Option now a days but its really Very Risky

Targeted

Apply Where 90 Days Works

Large MNCs, service companies, future project hiring, senior roles, niche skills, and referral openings can wait longer. But keep patience for this

Profile Power

Become Worth Waiting For

Strong resume, project clarity, communication, fundamentals, SQL, APIs, GitHub, portfolio, and mock interviews increase wait tolerance. Be Full-Stack ,AI Expert add skill which is in demand

Before Resigning

Non-Negotiable Checklist

Do not resign casually. Make sure the basics are ready before taking the risky path.

✓ Resume ATS ready
✓ LinkedIn / Naukri updated
✓ Core topics revised
✓ Mock interviews done
✓ 2-3 months savings
✓ No major financial pressure
✓ Scenario Based Questions
✓ Market demand checked

Do This, Not That

Smart Actions vs Common Mistakes

⚠️

Common Mistakes

Lying outright about notice period, resigning without preparation, ignoring buyout policy, applying randomly, and saying only "90 days" in the first recruiter call.

✅

Smart Actions

Use negotiable framing, ask for early release, target companies that can wait, use referrals, prepare before resigning, and discuss buyout reimbursement.

Final Message

90 Days Is A Constraint, Not A Career Stopper

Plan your profile, timing, negotiation, savings, and company targeting. The goal is simple: keep recruiter conversations open long enough for your skills to speak.
